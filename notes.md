# Beef & Guinness Stew — key techniques

Source: [W2 KITCHEN — Beef and Guinness Stew — Why It Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKIo0SOOW2E)

## Why Guinness behaves differently in the pot
- Drink cues (N₂ cream, dark roast, mild bitterness/acidity/sweetness) collapse when cooked: foam gone, roast-barley note dominates, alcohol & fruit fade, bitterness becomes louder.
- The stew’s job is to **pair that roast-bitter profile with beef**, not recreate a pint.

## Building the braise
1. **Sear in batches** on a hot, shimmering high-smoke oil — evaporate surface moisture so Maillard can meet the stout’s roast barley; juices concentrate into **fond**.
2. Prefer **chunkier cuts** so browning doesn’t overcook the interior; always let the pot recover heat between batches.
3. **Caramelize carrots & parsnips** first, then remove so they keep shape; their sugars later balance stout bitterness.
4. **Skim fat early** — late skimming strips fat-soluble aroma.
5. **Leeks** (in season) or onion + celery: salt, release moisture to **lift fond**, then evaporate to concentrate.
6. **Worcestershire** bridges the gap: anchovy (savoury), malt vinegar (bitterness + barley), tamarind (lost fruit), molasses (dark sweetness).
7. Deglaze with Guinness, return beef + juices to **gauge liquid** (avoid dilution). Optional chicken stock for body; **beef shin** preferred over chuck; bay + one prune optional (prune especially with less-sweet leeks).
8. **140°C / 2 h** covered, then garnish back **30–40 min** (optional lid-off). Further reduce **in a side pan** so meat stays soft and carrots stay crunchy.
